Store it. Lube it. Clean it. Keep it BitReady.

Patented Drill Bit Storage That Keeps Bits Ready to Cut

The BitReady Index is a patented drill bit storage, lubrication, and cleaning system designed to keep cutting tools organized, maintained, and ready before the first hole.

Licensing & Manufacturing Inquiries View Patent Details

The Problem

Drill bits are easy to lose, dull, dirty, and forget to lubricate.

01

Scattered bits

Bits get loose across tool bags, drawers, cases, trucks, and benches when the job is moving fast.

02

Dry cutting edges

Cutting edges wear faster when metal drilling happens dry or lubrication is skipped out of convenience.

03

Chips and debris

Dirty bits carry chips, dust, and residue into the next cut, the next case, or the next tool bag.

04

Storage only

Existing indexes mostly store bits. They do not help maintain the cutting end before and after use.

The Solution

The BitReady Index combines storage, lubrication, and cleaning in one compact case.

Instead of only holding bits, the BitReady Index is designed to store drill bits upright, allow the cutting ends to contact lubricant, provide a removable lubricant cup for dipping, and include a brush-style cleaning tube for debris removal.

The goal is practical: keep common bits where users expect them, help preserve cutting performance, and make basic maintenance part of the same workflow as storage.

System Features

A drill bit case designed around maintenance, not just storage.

Organized bit index

Dedicated bit positions support faster selection and cleaner storage.

Built-in lubricant reservoir

A reservoir concept keeps lubricant available where the bits already live.

Removable lube cup

A cup-style component supports controlled dipping and serviceability.

Integrated cleaning tube

A dedicated tube helps remove debris from the cutting end after use.

Internal bristles

Brush-style bristles are intended to help clear chips and residue.

Debris collection cup

Collected debris can be separated from stored bits and lubricant areas.

Protective lid

A lid concept helps protect contents during transport and storage.

Kickstand-style support

A support concept improves access on a bench, job cart, or work surface.

Jobsite and shop use

Built for trades, maintenance, metalwork, and serious home projects.
